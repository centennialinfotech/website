Digital Client Relations Manager

Digital Client Relations Manager

Be part of an innovative company that champions growth and excellence. This remote role offers the flexibility to experienced sales professionals and motivated newcomers alike. Enjoy exceptional support and maximize your earning potential in a fulfilling career path.

Key Responsibilities:
  • Collaborate with seasoned mentors and excel in a supportive team setting.
  • Initiate client interactions via phone to understand their needs.
  • Schedule and lead virtual consultations (via Zoom or phone) for comprehensive discussions.
  • Design personalized insurance quotes to meet each client's needs.
  • Present effective solutions to close sales and provide customized insurance plans.
What We Offer:
  • A flexible 3-4 day workweek for better work-life balance.
  • Free, extensive online training and continuous support.
  • Work exclusively with warm leads, no cold calling necessary.
  • Rapid commission payouts with minimal waiting periods.
  • State-of-the-art sales technology provided at no cost.
  • Ongoing professional development with mentorship from industry leaders.
  • All-expenses-paid annual incentive trips to top global destinations.
This is a 1099 commission-based role specializing in financial products such as IULs, annuities, and life insurance, perfect for those looking to expand their expertise and impact.

Ready to transform your sales career? Apply now and join a team dedicated to your professional growth and success .

Application Notice: U.S. Candidates Only

We are currently accepting applications only from candidates residing in the United States. Please note that any applications submitted from outside the United States will not be considered, and no further notification will be provided regarding the rejection.
